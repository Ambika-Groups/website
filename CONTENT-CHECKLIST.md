## Photos: how many, and what makes a good one

**General rules for every photo**
- Send the **original file** from the phone or camera. Any modern phone is fine. We resize the photos automatically, so don't shrink or edit them.
- **Minimum size: 2000 pixels on the long side.** Any phone photo taken in the last 5 years is bigger than this.
- **Landscape (wider than tall)** unless noted below. Hold the phone sideways.
- Take them **in daylight**, with the **finished work clearly visible** and in focus.
- **No watermarks, text, logos or collages** on the photo.
- **People:** only with their permission. No children's faces. Workers wearing safety gear look professional.
- **Location data** saved in photos is removed automatically before they go online.

| Photo | How many | Shape | What makes a good shot | File name to use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Home page main photo** | 1 | Landscape | Your best finished project, from a wide angle. The left side may be cropped on some screens, so keep the main subject in the centre. | `hero.jpg` |
| **Service cover photos** | 4 (one per service) | Landscape | **Construction:** a finished building, front view. **Road works:** a finished road, looking along it. **Interlocking:** paver work close enough to see the pattern. **Real estate:** your project building or site. | `service-construction.jpg`, `service-road-works.jpg`, `service-interlocking-paver.jpg`, `service-real-estate-development.jpg` |
| **Service galleries** | 3 or more per service | Landscape | Different jobs, or different stages of one job (before / during / after). | `service-road-works-1.jpg`, `service-road-works-2.jpg`, … (same pattern for each service) |
| **Project photos** | 1 main + 3 to 8 per project | Landscape | Main photo: the finished result, wide. Others: details, other angles, before/after. | `project-<short-name>-1.jpg` (main), `-2.jpg`, `-3.jpg`, … We'll give you each project's short name. |
| **About page** | 1 | Landscape | Your team on site, your office, or a signature project. | `about.jpg` |
| **Leadership** (only if shown) | 1 per person | **Square or portrait** | Head and shoulders, plain background, good light, at least 800 × 800 pixels. | `team-1.jpg`, `team-2.jpg` (in the order listed on the About page) |

If renaming files is a hassle, just send the photos with a note saying which is which, and we'll name them.
